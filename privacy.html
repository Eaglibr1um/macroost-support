MacRoost Privacy Policy

Local data

MacRoost stores daily notes, imported files, preferences and recovery snapshots locally on your device. It does not send this content to Jax, use analytics, display ads, create app accounts or track you.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off until enabled. Daily note versions, task text, relationships between versions and a library identifier are stored in your private CloudKit database, operated by Apple. Jax does not receive these notes. Attachments, preferences and local recovery files are not synced. Turning sync off keeps existing copies.

Removing text from the current note does not erase older versions. You may keep cloud history until manually removed, or choose 7, 30 or 90 day cleanup. Current notes, unresolved drafts and at least 20 recent versions per day are kept. Cleanup removes old draft text; small records containing version identifiers, dates and relationships remain to prevent offline devices from restoring removed text. Cloud history pauses at its 100 MB note-text limit.

Remove All iCloud Notes & History removes the cloud note zone, including draft text and version-removal records. Current notes and local recovery copies remain on devices. A small library status record stays in your private database to prevent automatic re-upload. A new library requires an explicit choice. An Apple account change pauses sync instead of automatically transferring notes.

Optional AI features

On Mac, external AI connections are disabled by default. A client with a connection key can read daily notes and tasks. Read-only is the default, and edits require approval. A connected AI service may send what it reads to its model provider under that provider’s policies. Keys are stored in macOS Keychain; connection names and permissions are local. Activity records contain action names and outcomes, not note content, and are held in memory. You can revoke connections or disable access. Built-in summaries use Apple’s on-device model when available, without a cloud-model fallback.

Exports, links and recovery

You choose where to export notes. If the destination is managed by a cloud or backup service, that service’s policies apply. Local recovery snapshots are separate from cloud history. External links open in your browser, where the website’s policies apply. Interface icons are bundled locally.

Support email

Contact Support opens a draft and sends nothing automatically. Your email provider and Jax’s email provider process information you choose to send. Jax uses it to answer your request. Do not include private notes unless needed. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact above; email providers may retain their own copies under their policies.

Removing local data

Uninstalling may leave local data behind. On Mac, use Open Notes Folder to locate notes and recovery files; quit the app before removing its complete data folder. On iPhone, deleting the app removes its local app data. Removing local files does not automatically remove cloud history; use the separate iCloud removal control.

This support website and purchases

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests, including visitor IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy policy. These pages use no analytics, cookies added by Jax, advertising or external fonts. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple’s privacy policy. MacRoost does not receive payment card details.

Changes

Updates will be reflected in the date on this page and in the policy supplied with future app versions.
